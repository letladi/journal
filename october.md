## 21 October 2025

On Sunday I had what one could call an epiphany, which is basically that trading is a business and has to be thought of in those terms. Like any business it needs a good strategy (a profitable one) and must manage its costs. But like any trading business, it must sell goods for cheap and sell them with high enough margins to more than break-even, it must make profit.

Like any business it needs a specialization/niche and it also needs working hours and ways of working.
I don't want to stretch the metaphor too much, but the 'trading as a business' language resonates with me more than trading as a game. But the real implications is not semantics it's the idea that if I enter a trade, I should not get out at 1R, but instead must aim for 3-4R, that's the margin of my business.
